# v8 (keep every phase start, budget curve) against v5 at a $0.50 cap

## Context

Built by the "Spend per game" thread on branch `claude/project-thread-9j4kxi` at 0fdcf57 (code 18a2d77), with
`CAPTURE_REVIEW=0.67,PRESS_UPDATE_BRIEFINGS=1,GOVERNOR_KEEP_STARTS=1,GOVERNOR_RESERVE_USD=0.02` on the clean
player base, and uploaded by the release thread as `scott-pidip:v8` (2fba209d-fdff-4988-a6fb-e448cfddf631). Scott
asked for a budget that follows a curve over the game and to "error on the side of under-squeezing where
possible". In one local game to 1908 at a $0.50 cap against six Luna seats, v8 spent $0.474, completed 16 of 16
phase starts on Sol, ran 17 press wakes and held back 12, with 0 refused calls and 0 rejected orders; v5 with no
governor in the same setup had every call refused from turn 9.

Earlier record `2026-10-14-v6-luna-press-at-050.md` (inconclusive, -0.052 ± 0.043 for v6): v5 was refused by
the cap from turn 7 in all 49 seats and still scored above v6. That is a prior that late-game model use is
worth less in this field than expected. Checked `closed_levers.md` (empty) and `open_levers.md` (the v6 entry).

## Predictions — pre-registered

- **If TRUE we expect:** v8 minus v5 at least +0.03 a game, with v8 completing its phase starts through 1908
  (`wake` lines of kind phase_start every movement phase) and v5 refused from mid-game as in the v6 run.
- **If FALSE we expect:** a difference within ±0.03, or v8 below v5. That would say again that v5's lost
  late-game model calls cost it little in this field.

## Design

Paired, balanced (`run_ab.py`, 14 bodies x 5 = 70 games), field set `league-top5-2026-10-14`, every seat capped
at $0.50. Five a body because the v6 run lost 9 of 56 games to platform pod failures. Read beside the score:
phase starts completed per seat, seat spend (at or below $0.48), `wake_skipped` with reason `start_reserve`,
and refused calls. Launch after the v7 run (`2026-10-15-hybrid-diplodocus-vs-v5`) and after v8's health game.
