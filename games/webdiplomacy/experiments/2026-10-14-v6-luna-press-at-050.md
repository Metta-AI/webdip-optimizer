# v6 (Luna for press wakes, spend squeeze) against v5 at a $0.50 cap

## Context

The league's per-seat model cap was $2.0 at 23:47Z on 2026-10-14 (`episode_player_pod_llm_spend_limit_usd`)
and is expected to drop to $0.50. v5's governor budget is a fixed $2. The "Spend per game" thread built v6 on
its branch `claude/project-thread-9j4kxi` at 658d18b. Uploaded by the release thread as `scott-pidip:v6` (title
"Squeezed press, Luna wakes, for the $0.50 cap"). In one local game to 1908, the seat spent $0.32 and the governor
never cut back. v5 as-is spent $0.45 and was cut back in all 16 phases (that thread's README table).

Health game: xreq_03dd6517 (classic-press-short, all seven seats v6, $0.50 a seat; run and read by the
"Spend per game" thread): 185 of 185 model calls 200, all on Sol; 0 rejected orders; every seat read a $0.50 limit
from the hosted `GET /spend` and stayed at full; $0.10 to $0.125 a seat. No press wake ran (every seat was Ready
before the 20 s debounce), so the Luna press path is untested hosted. The platform's sidecar allowlist is `null`
in metta's deployed values (`devops/app-manifests/values.yaml`), which admits any OpenRouter slug. Check v6's
`llm_call` lines on `openai/gpt-6-luna` in the first paired games; non-200s there count as failed calls.

## Predictions — pre-registered

- **If TRUE we expect:** v6 minus v5 at least +0.03 a game, with v5's governor or the sidecar's 429s cutting
  v5's press in mid-game and v6 at full press throughout (`press_behaviour.py`, `wd.py costs`).
- **If FALSE we expect:** a difference within ±0.03, or v6 below v5. That would mean Luna's press or the
  squeeze costs as much as v5's lost late-game press.

## Design

Paired, balanced (`run_ab.py`, 14 bodies x 4 = 56 games), field set `league-top5-2026-10-14` (five strongest
current entrants by league mean). Every seat, rivals included, is capped at $0.50, as in the league after the
drop. Confound: rivals' own spend behaviour at $0.50 may differ from what they do in the league at $2; the A/B
difference is still fair because both of our seats face the same games.

## Result

56 games launched at 23:55Z on 2026-10-14, all back by 00:56Z. 3 never started (`player_never_started`:
Kubernetes scheduled no player pod before the connect timeout) and 3 more lost a seat pod (`pod_deleted`), so
`paired.py` read **47 clean pairs**, 25 of 47 with v6 in the lower slot.

- **v6 minus v5: -0.052 a game, SE 0.043, z -1.2** (SD 0.29). Single-version reads: v6 0.229 a game (+0.105
  over field par, n 50), v5 0.262 (+0.145, n 49). Final centres 6.84 against 7.51; survival 0.96 against 1.00.
- By power (v6 minus v5 against par): Austria -0.16, England -0.10, France -0.11, Turkey -0.18, Germany +0.04,
  Italy +0.04, Russia +0.05; n 5 to 8 a cell, every cell |z| < 2.
- **The mechanism fired as predicted.** v5 hit the $0.50 cap in all 49 seats: 2,519 of its 8,329 model calls
  were refused, first at turn 7 (median; range 5 to 12), and its governor stayed at full all game because it
  plans for $2. v6 spent $0.24 a seat on average (its governor's own count), never cut back, and 1 of its 5,674
  calls failed (0.02%). Luna answered on the hosted route (`pidip_model` switches each press wake, 200s).
- **Press behaviour** (`press_behaviour.py`, 706 v6 and 722 v5 movement phases): v6 committed a policy in 99% of
  phases against 47% for v5, but its policies said less: orders fixed 59% against 89%, every unit fixed 44%
  against 81%, a province forbidden 44% against 90%, expected orders listed 18% against 69%. Messages a phase
  3.87 against 3.08. Model cost a phase $0.016 against $0.029.
- 0 rejected orders for either version. Both log 1 to 4 webDiplomacy `http_error` 400s a game.

## Verdict

**inconclusive**, and the release rule fails: v6 does not replace v5. The rule asks for v6 minus v5 of at
least +0.03 with t at least 1 over 50+ clean pairs; the run read -0.052 (z -1.2) over 47. The sign is against
the hypothesis, but |z| < 2 against zero and the run could only have shown an effect of about 0.09 at z = 2.

The predicted cause of v5's loss was real (its model access stopped at turn 7 in every game) and it did not
cost v5 the game. Two readings, neither tested: v5's detailed early-game policies are worth more than late
press, or v6's Luna wakes commit thinner policies (the behaviour table) that cost what its later press gains.
v6 also left about half of its $0.50 unspent. Entered in `open_levers.md`.
