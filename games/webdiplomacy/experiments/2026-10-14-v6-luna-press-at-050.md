# v6 (Luna for press wakes, spend squeeze) against v5 at a $0.50 cap

## Context

The league's per-seat model cap was $2.0 at 23:47Z on 2026-10-14 (`episode_player_pod_llm_spend_limit_usd`)
and is expected to drop to $0.50. v5's governor budget is a fixed $2. The "Spend per game" thread built v6 on
its branch `claude/project-thread-9j4kxi` at 658d18b. Uploaded by the release thread as `scott-pidip:v6` (title
"Squeezed press, Luna wakes, for the $0.50 cap"). In one local game to 1908, the seat spent $0.32 and the governor
never cut back. v5 as-is spent $0.45 and was cut back in all 16 phases (that thread's README table).

## Predictions — pre-registered

- **If TRUE we expect:** v6 minus v5 at least +0.03 a game, with v5's governor or the sidecar's 429s cutting
  v5's press in mid-game and v6 at full press throughout (`press_behaviour.py`, `wd.py costs`).
- **If FALSE we expect:** a difference within ±0.03, or v6 below v5. That would mean Luna's press or the
  squeeze costs as much as v5's lost late-game press.

## Design

Paired, balanced (`run_ab.py`, 14 bodies x 4 = 56 games), field set `league-top5-2026-10-14` (five strongest
current entrants by league mean). Every seat, rivals included, is capped at $0.50, as in the league after the
drop. Confound: rivals' own spend behaviour at $0.50 may differ from what they do in the league at $2; the A/B
difference is still fair because both of our seats face the same games.
